# Built-in brain: credits and licence

`brain-fine.bin`, `brain-medium.bin` and `brain-broad.bin` are based on "Brain Model" by Nevit Dilmen, published on NIH 3D (entry 3DPX-002739, https://3d.nih.gov/entries/3dpx-002739) under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes from the original:
- turned Y-up and scaled to 2.4 units across;
- the cerebellum and brainstem removed;
- each hemisphere filled and smoothed on a voxel grid, so the folds are gone;
- the hemispheres joined by a corpus callosum;
- the surface rebuilt at 0.045, 0.055 and 0.065 unit spacing;
- turned a quarter turn about the vertical, so its side faces the app's starting camera.
